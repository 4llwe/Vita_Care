# Security and Go-live gates

Mandatory: dual approval for EWS protocols; audited break-glass with ≤60 minute expiry; read-access audit; notification outbox delivery review; dependency lockfile; migration dry-run and restore rehearsal; SAST/dependency/container scans; penetration test; mobile/desktop accessibility QA; provider failover simulation; clinical deterioration tabletop; privacy impact assessment; signed clinical and regulatory approval.

No real patient data is permitted until every gate has attached evidence and an accountable approver.
